Target audience

Highlight:

This work is written for the chief transformation officer of a large U.S. bank who chairs the executive steering committee for a multi-year cloud and data program. At the end of Q4 the committee must decide whether the program moves from Walk to Run. The chair has to show which initiatives are driving any delay, who is accountable for each funding and architecture decision, and whether the organization, not only the technology, is ready to scale.

What we added

7initiatives sequenced
7recurring decisions with one owner
8leading and lagging metrics
5Capital One sources

We treated this as a governance problem as much as a technology plan. Instead of listing initiatives, we sequenced them by dependency, so you can see how cloud migration, application modernization and the data ecosystem make Eno expansion and Slingshot possible, and what slips if one of them does. We built it from Capital One's own annual report and engineering articles, and labeled each dependency as documented or proposed, so you can tell your committee which parts are evidence and which are our judgment. The dashboard pairs leading and lagging indicators, links key metrics to the decisions they inform, and defines measures the sources do not publish, such as time to data access and whether the Eno Product Lead and Slingshot GM roles are filled. Our team also brings practitioner experience from corporate treasury work with banking partners on long-term enterprise projects and from a cross-functional digital system rollout, which informs our choice to give each recurring decision one accountable owner and to track staffing, not only milestones.
